FAQ

AI 配对报告是什么？要钱吗？What is the AI match report? Is it free?

AI 报告百分百准确吗？Is the AI report 100% accurate?

不是。报告根据候选人自己提供的履历做比对，是帮您看得更清楚的工具。重点资料要在面试时核实，最后由您决定。No. It is based on the biodata the candidate provides and helps you see more clearly. Key details are verified at interview, and the final decision is yours.
